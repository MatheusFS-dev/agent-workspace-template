# Case: Explain why or how something happens

Cover these in order. Each item is a short paragraph unless marked.

1. Open with the short answer.
2. The mechanism from cause to result, with a diagram when it has three or
   more steps.
3. One small concrete case with real numbers or values that shows the
   mechanism at work.
4. A common misunderstanding and its correction, if there is a relevant one.
