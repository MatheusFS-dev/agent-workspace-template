# Case: Understand a paper, method, equation, or research concept

Cover these in order. Each item is a short paragraph unless marked.

1. Open with what the method or concept does in one sentence. Then state the
   problem it solves and why the usual approach was not enough.
2. How it works in three to six steps, with a diagram of the flow. Present
   each core equation once, then explain its symbols and what it computes.
   Skip equations that the idea does not need.
3. A small worked example with real numbers that can be followed by hand.
4. What is new compared with the usual approach, and the assumptions or limits
   the paper states.
5. The section, figure, or equation numbers that match each step, so the user
   can map the explanation back to the paper.

Saved report headings: "How it works" and "Worked example".

Ground every claim in the paper text. Mark your own interpretation with "In
my reading" or a similar phrase. If the paper or a needed part of it is not
available, say so instead of guessing.
