Make each unit answer its heading: state the main pattern, support it with a few decisive values or figure references, compare relevant conditions, and close with the finding the reader should retain. Use tables for complete values and prose for interpretation. Do not replace requested case-level analyses with an aggregate overview or a table.

Separate descriptive changes from statistical conclusions. Name the comparison and its units, and do not imply significance without the specified inference. Report material variation or exceptions where they change the finding.
