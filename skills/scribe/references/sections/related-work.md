# Related Work

Load this file only for a Related Work section.

Connect the discussion to the gap established in the Introduction. Organize prior work into two to four technical categories grouped by question or mechanism. For each category:

1. summarize the shared mechanism or assumption,
2. identify the limitation relevant to the paper's target challenge,
3. explain the paper's exact distinction.

Within those categories, discuss each close work's mechanism, study scope, and relation to the present question in prose. Give closest works more space than distant context. If used, a comparison table summarizes the discussion rather than replacing it. Different objectives are distinctions, not automatic deficiencies.

End with a positioning statement naming the closest relation and the supported mechanism, assumption, scope, or evidence difference. Do not organize only chronologically, give equal space to weak and close work, hide strong baselines, or produce a citation dump. Novelty cannot follow merely from different wording. Related-work claims require supplied citations, and missing citations must not be invented.
