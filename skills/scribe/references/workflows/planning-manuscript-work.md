# Planning Manuscript Work

> **Supporting module:** Part of the single `scribe-superpowers` skill. Load it only after explicit approval of a manuscript-scale design or when the user explicitly requests a plan.

## Purpose

Convert the approved design into an executable section-level plan without reconstructing or expanding the approved scope.

## Plan Contract

Record:

- goal, audience or venue, central thesis, evidence boundary, output format, and design source,
- facts, numbers, citations, terminology, acronyms, notation, claim qualifiers, and known limitations that must remain stable,
- a claim-evidence matrix with allowed wording, supporting evidence, scope qualifier, and destination,
- a section map defining each section's role, opening claim, inputs, evidence, limitations, terminology, dependencies, and completion criteria.

Use `unsupported, do not claim` when evidence is absent. Do not write placeholders that invite invention.

## Task Shape

Each task produces one coherent, independently reviewable section, subsection, response unit, or diagnostic artifact. State exact inputs, output, dependencies, applicable section reference, allowed claims, and verification criteria.

Each drafting task must include:

1. read the named inputs,
2. extract only relevant evidence,
3. draft to the section role and claim boundary,
4. check the claim-evidence matrix,
5. apply one revision through `quality-control.md`,
6. verify preservation, terminology, citations, and format,
7. record unresolved evidence gaps without inventing content.

## Plan Failures

Reject plans containing `TBD`, generic `add evidence`, unidentified citations, overlapping section responsibilities, undefined terminology or notation, unsupported claims, or instructions such as `write an appropriate discussion` without defining the argument.

Before handoff, verify design coverage, claim coverage, consistency, evidence boundaries, task sizing, and absence of placeholders. Then use `executing-writing-plans.md` when execution is requested.
