# Responding to Reviewers

> **Supporting module:** Part of the single `scribe-superpowers` skill. Load it for response letters and point-by-point rebuttals.

## Core Rule

Answer the actual comment directly, ground every statement in the manuscript or supplied evidence, and distinguish completed changes from proposed changes. Reviewer authority does not authorize unsupported claims or changes that contradict the work.

## Process

For each comment:

1. Restate or quote only enough of the comment to identify the issue.
2. Classify it as clarification, correction, evidence request, scope concern, methodological disagreement, presentation issue, or misunderstanding.
3. Determine what the existing evidence supports.
4. Choose one response position: agree, partly agree, clarify, or respectfully disagree.
5. State the action taken and identify the affected section, figure, table, equation, or wording when known.
6. Do not claim that a manuscript change was made unless it was actually applied. Otherwise label it as proposed.

## Response Shape

A strong response normally contains:

- acknowledgment of the concrete concern,
- direct answer,
- evidence or reasoning,
- precise manuscript change or justified decision not to change,
- scoped closing statement.

Keep the tone respectful and technical. Avoid flattery, defensiveness, claims about reviewer intent, and vague phrases such as `the manuscript has been improved accordingly` without identifying the change.

## Disagreement

When disagreeing:

- identify the factual or interpretive point,
- cite the relevant manuscript evidence,
- acknowledge any wording that could have caused the misunderstanding,
- revise ambiguous wording when useful,
- preserve the scientific position when the comment conflicts with the evidence.

## Cross-Response Consistency

Check that separate responses do not contradict one another, promise incompatible changes, use different terminology, or broaden the manuscript beyond its evidence. Load `references/reviewer-derived-writing-notes.md` or one topical reviewer example only when relevant.

Apply `quality-control.md` after the response draft.
